    Cases

    Order sets load order: 1 goes in first, at the nose. Give "first off at the venue" gear the highest number.

    Sample job loaded so you can see how it works. Replace it with your own cases, or press New.

    Load bars and straps

    Each one goes across the truck right behind that order group, and the next group loads behind it. Load bars take 3" of floor, straps 1". To put one in the middle of a group, split those cases into two order numbers.

    Packing rules
    Set every case to sit on:

    Cases stay upright (flight cases are never laid on their sides). "Can turn sideways" lets a case rotate 90° on the floor. "Same case only" is for cases with caster cups in the lid: a case sits only on an identical case, lined up so its wheels drop into the cups. "Max stack" is how many cases high, counting the bottom one; blank means as high as fits. In a mixed stack the lowest limit wins.
